FAQ

Questions before outsourcing bookkeeping.

Clear answers about scope, handoffs and what this service does not claim to provide.

What does Multigain In do?+

Multigain In is presented as a remote bookkeeping and finance-operations practice focused on monthly books, cleanup, reporting and bookkeeping systems.

Do you file taxes?+

This prototype does not present Multigain In as a tax preparer or CPA firm. Tax returns and tax advice should be handled by appropriately qualified professionals.

Can you clean up overdue books?+

Yes. Catch-up and historical cleanup are included as dedicated services in this rebuilt site.

Which bookkeeping platforms do you support?+

The site is structured around common cloud bookkeeping workflows such as QuickBooks Online and Xero. Final supported systems should be verified before launch.

How does onboarding work?+

A typical flow is discovery, access review, bookkeeping assessment, scope confirmation, cleanup if needed, then recurring close.

Can you work with my CPA?+

Yes. The service catalog includes CPA collaboration and year-end books readiness support.

Do you offer payroll?+

The site describes payroll coordination and payroll journal review, not acting as a payroll provider unless the business actually offers that service.

Are the prices final?+

No. Pricing on this prototype is sample pricing for demonstration and should be replaced with verified commercial terms.
